### Events

Stuff happens while time passes.

### Traits

Traits are permanent character attributes. There are positive and negative traits.
Most negative traits are applied during gameplay.

Positive:
* Deadeye: expert marksman.
* Scout: sees further on the map.
* Survivalist: bonuses to camp activities.

Negative:
* Missing limbs: depending on limbs, can reduce speed, dexterity, or disable the use of some items.
